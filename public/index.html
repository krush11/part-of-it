Part‑of‑It‑Verse

A certain Managerial Ethics professor has a catchphrase. It is not a filler word. It is not a verbal tic. It is a way of life.

Utilitarianism? Part of it. Stakeholder theory? Part of it. The reason you are reading this instead of the pre‑read? Also, somehow, part of it.

This website exists because one of us tried to count by hand and lost track somewhere around the Kantian slide. Science demands a better instrument. This is that instrument.

How it works

  1. 🔊
    He says it. You tap.
    That is the whole job. No counting, no remembering, no spreadsheet.
  2. 🧠
    The class decides, not you.
    A "part of it" only counts when enough of you tap at the same moment. One person mashing is a cry for attention, not a data point.
  3. 🏆
    Earn a title.
    Bat Ears. Back Bencher. Trigger Happy. Your ears, ranked, privately. Nobody else sees your name.
